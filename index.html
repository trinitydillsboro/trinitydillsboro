Worship

Our services follow the historic liturgy of the church from Lutheran Service Book, the hymnal we use every Sunday. The bulletin has the order of service, so you can follow along, sing, or simply listen.

The Lord's Supper

In the Lord's Supper, Jesus gives His true body and blood, in, with, and under the bread and wine, for the forgiveness of sins. He said so Himself: “Take, eat; this is my body,” and “this is my blood of the covenant, which is poured out for many for the forgiveness of sins” (Matthew 26:26, 28 ESV).

Because receiving the Supper together is also a confession of one faith, Trinity practices closed communion, in keeping with the practice of the Lutheran Church—Missouri Synod. If you are not a member of a Missouri Synod congregation, please speak with Pastor Richardson before the service, and he will gladly talk with you about the Supper and what we believe.

Beliefs

What We Believe

Trinity is a congregation of the Lutheran Church—Missouri Synod. We believe the Holy Scriptures are the Word of God, and we confess their teaching as it is summarized in the Lutheran Confessions, gathered in the Book of Concord of 1580.

God's Law shows us our sin: we have not loved God with our whole heart or our neighbor as ourselves, and we cannot save ourselves. So God sent His Son, Jesus Christ, true God and true man, who died on the cross for the sins of the whole world and rose again on the third day. For Jesus' sake God forgives sinners freely.

“For by grace you have been saved through faith. And this is not your own doing; it is the gift of God, not a result of works, so that no one may boast.”

Ephesians 2:8–9 (ESV)

God gives this forgiveness through the means He has chosen: the preaching of His Word, Holy Baptism, and the Lord's Supper. In Baptism He washes us and makes us His children, and in the Supper He feeds us with the true body and blood of His Son. That is why we gather every Sunday: to hear Him speak and to receive what He gives.

Pastor and Office

Rev. Kyle Richardson, Pastor

Pastor Kyle Richardson was installed as Trinity's pastor on October 3, 2026. He attended Ball State University in Muncie, Indiana, for his undergraduate studies and then Concordia Theological Seminary in Fort Wayne. He was ordained on June 30, 2019, at St. John Lutheran Church in Island Lake, Illinois.

He translated Gottfried Thomasius's 1857 work on the Lutheran confession of the atonement for the 2025 volume Atonement in Confessional Lutheran Theology: Harnack, Thomasius, and Philippi.
